Trending engine

Know why a clip is about to pop.

View counts alone don't tell you much. ClipHuntr snapshots every clip as it grows and scores it on what matters:

  • Breakout: how far above the streamer's normal this clip is. A small streamer's 10× clip beats a big streamer's average one.
  • Velocity & acceleration: views per hour right now, and whether it's speeding up because people are sharing it.
  • 24h projection: where the clip is heading, so you can grab it while it's still rising.
  • Viral score: all of it blended into one 0–100 number you can sort by.

FAQ

Questions

Who is ClipHuntr for?

Clippers and clipping channels: people who find great moments from other people's streams and post them as Shorts, TikToks and Reels. It isn't a tool for streamers to clip themselves.

Do I need Twitch or Kick API keys?

No. ClipHuntr works out of the box. You can add your own Twitch developer credentials in Settings if you want, but you don't need to.

Which platforms does it track?

Twitch and Kick, side by side. You can track any streamer on either, see who's live right now, and filter by game, language, length and more.

Does it run in the cloud?

No. It runs on your own computer (or an Android phone), so your clips, lists and data stay with you. Open the dashboard from any browser on your network.

Why does Windows or macOS warn me when I install it?

The installers aren't code-signed yet. On Windows, click More info → Run anyway. On macOS, right-click the app and choose Open the first time.

What does the Android app do?

It turns an Android phone into an always-on ClipHuntr server: scanning, trending, alerts and downloads. You use the dashboard from your computer's browser. Editing and rendering need the desktop app.

When is Linux coming?

Soon. Until then, Windows, macOS and Android are all available.
